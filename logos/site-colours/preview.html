Open-source retro gaming frontend

All your retro games, everywhere.

NeoStation turns your ROM collection into one library you play with a controller, on your PC, your TV and your handheld.

ADownload for freeRead the guideFree · GPL-3.0

Set up

Point it at your ROMs. That is the whole setup.

  1. 01Pick your ROMs folderOne folder with a subfolder per system.
  2. 02Let it scan119 systems detected, artwork fetched.
  3. 03Press AGames open in RetroArch or your emulator.

Free and open source · GPL-3.0

Free to use, free to read, free to change.
